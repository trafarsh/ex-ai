Shot Exporter

Your cuts are already on the timeline. Export each one.

Shot Exporter reads the clip edges on V1 and renders every clip as its own file. It never guesses where the cuts are, because you already made them.

  1. Read timelineEvery clip on V1 is listed with its in, out and frame count.
  2. Tick the shotsPick all of them, none, or just the clips selected in your Timeline.
  3. ExportQueue them in Media Encoder and keep editing, or render straight from Premiere.
  • Frame-exactIn and out are set from Premiere's own clip ticks, not rounded seconds.
  • Any presetH.264, ProRes, HEVC or any saved .epr export preset.
  • Your names{seq}_shot_{n}, plus clip name and timecode tokens.
  • Leaves no traceYour sequence's In and Out marks are put back afterwards.

Make pack

Turn a movie into a scenepack.

Face grouping runs on your computer. Your footage is never uploaded, and the panel never tries to identify real people.

FAQ

Questions

How do I install it?

Unzip the download, close Premiere and run the installer: install-windows.bat on Windows, install-mac.sh on Mac. Then open Premiere and go to Window → Extensions.

Does Shot Exporter detect scenes?

No. It exports the cuts you made on V1, clip by clip, at their exact frames. If your movie isn't cut yet, Edit Suite's Make pack tab can cut it with Premiere's own Scene Edit Detection.

Can it do real speed ramps?

Not automatically. Premiere gives extensions no way to change clip speed or Time Remapping. Velocity punch adds the scale-and-blur hit on each beat, and you add the speed change by hand.

Is my footage uploaded anywhere?

No. Face grouping in Make pack runs on your computer. The panel only goes online to check for new looks, trending sounds and the scenepack site list, and when you load a cast list.

How do the looks update themselves?

The panel checks for new looks when it opens and every few hours, then adds them to the Color tab. You never reinstall for new looks.

Can I sell or post scenepacks I make?

Scenepacks are clips from films and shows owned by their studios. Use them for fan edits, and check the rules of the platform you post to before using them commercially.

Does it work with Premiere in another language?

Premiere in English is supported. Some effect names differ in other languages, so a few effects may not apply there.
