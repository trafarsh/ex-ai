9:16 for TikTok

Fill scales every clip so it covers the vertical frame. Keyframed scale is left alone.

Workflow

  1. Setup: make the sequence 9:16 and fill the frame.
  2. Beats: enter the song's BPM, put the playhead on the first beat, create beat markers.
  3. Cut: cut your clips on the beats, or lay selected clips one per beat.
  4. Effects, Text, Color: everything lands on the beat markers.
  5. Export: render each clip on V1 as its own file.

Beat markers from BPM

Markers are named “Beat 1, Beat 2…”, every bar's first beat is red. Your own markers are never touched.

Don't know the BPM?

Tapped: – BPM · play the song and tap at least 8 times.

    Cut clips on the beat

    Splits every clip on the track at each beat it spans. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes.

    Beat montage

    Select clips in the Project panel, put the playhead where the montage starts. One clip per beat(s), in the order you selected them.

    Keep your music on A1; the clips' own sound goes on the track you pick, which must be empty there.

    Effects on the beat

    About real speed ramps: Premiere does not let any extension change clip speed or time-remap keyframes, so a true velocity edit still needs Time Remapping by hand (right-click clip → Show Clip Keyframes → Time Remapping → Speed). “Velocity punch” gives the look on the beat: a scale rush with motion and directional blur.

    Text

    Words start at the playhead's next beat. Put text on V2 or higher, above your footage.

    CC looks

    Tip: select one adjustment layer over the whole edit to grade everything at once. Applying another look replaces the previous one on that clip.

    CC flicker on the beat

    Exposure/contrast/saturation flick with warm-cold alternation on each beat marker.
